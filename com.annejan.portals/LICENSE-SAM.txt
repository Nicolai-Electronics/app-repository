SAM (Software Automatic Mouth), as in third_party/sam/

SAM was published in 1982 by Don't Ask Software as a commercial program
for the Commodore 64, the Atari 8-bit and the Apple II. The C code here is
Sebastian Macke's reverse-engineered port of it
(https://github.com/s-macke/SAM, commit a7b36ef), with the changes listed
in third_party/sam/README.md.

No license has been granted for it. Don't Ask Software no longer exists,
attempts to reach the original authors have failed, and the author of the
port states that he cannot put it under any license: by his account it is
abandonware. It is included here as it is, for GLaDOS's voice, and is NOT
covered by the MIT license of the rest of this project.

If you hold rights to SAM and want it removed, open an issue at
https://github.com/annejan/tanmatsu-portals-grace/issues and it will be.
